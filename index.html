THE PERSON BEHIND MEMORY

Sushil Kumar Tiwari.

OWNER · MEMORY GROUP

Sushil Kumar Tiwari brings a hospitality-focused approach to Memory Group, with his professional work in stay support services in Patna.

For him, hospitality is not only about providing a room. It is about creating an environment built on discipline, dignity, cleanliness and genuine care.

A devoted Krishna bhakt , he draws inspiration from the values of humility, discipline, service and responsibility. These principles guide the way he looks at hospitality — maintaining discipline across the services while making every guest feel respected, comfortable and at home.
