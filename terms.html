01 · Accommodation

Memory Group provides hostel and PG accommodation subject to room availability, applicable pricing and the terms communicated at the time of joining.

02 · Pricing

The standard pricing displayed on the Memory Group website is provided for information and may be subject to room availability and property-specific arrangements.

  • ₹5,500 per bed per month for double-bed accommodation in 2 BHK.
  • ₹6,000 per bed per month for 6–7 sharing rooms.
  • ₹7,500 per bed per month for double-sharing rooms.
  • ₹12,500 per month for a single room.

03 · Security Deposit

A security deposit is applicable according to the accommodation category.

  • ₹3,000 security deposit for sharing rooms.
  • ₹5,000 security deposit for single rooms.

The applicable security deposit is adjustable against the final month's dues, subject to applicable deductions and settlement of outstanding amounts.

04 · Joining Fee

A one-time joining fee of ₹500 is applicable at the time of joining, unless otherwise communicated by Memory Group.

  • Any damage to property may be subject to applicable charges.
  • Residents must follow the rules and guidelines communicated by the respective hostel.

06 · Availability

Displayed room categories and prices do not guarantee availability. Final room allocation is subject to confirmation by Memory Group.

07 · Changes

Memory Group may update accommodation pricing, facilities, policies or these terms from time to time. The applicable terms at the time of joining will be communicated to the resident.

08 · Contact

For questions regarding accommodation, pricing, deposits or other terms, residents may contact Memory Group directly.
